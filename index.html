Simple Buying Guide for Gold & Silver in India

Look for BIS Hallmark

Always check for the 6-digit HUID code and BIS Hallmark logo on gold jewelry.

Understand Gold Purity

24K is 99.9% pure (coins/bars), 22K is 91.6% pure (standard jewelry), 18K is 75% pure.

GST & Making Charges

Standard GST on gold across India is 3%. Making charges usually vary between 8% to 18%.
